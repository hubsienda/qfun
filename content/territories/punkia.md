Punkia mocks corporate fog, AI theatre, LinkedIn rituals, fake innovation, management language, meritocracy myths, productivity cults, and institutional absurdity.

It is where polished nonsense goes after Proteus has finished poking it with a stick.

## What Punkia destroys

### Corporate fog

The language that hides simple confusion inside expensive vocabulary.

### AI theatre

The habit of calling everything intelligent because a pitch deck became nervous.

### LinkedIn rituals

The daily performance of humility, disruption, gratitude, resilience, and strategic fog.

### Fake innovation

Old nonsense wearing a new badge and asking for budget.

### Meritocracy myths

The pleasant story that every outcome is deserved, clean, rational, and morally tidy.

### Management language

The ceremonial phrases used to make obvious things sound unavailable to ordinary thought.

### Productivity cults

The belief that being busy is proof of importance.

### Institutional absurdity

The systems everyone obeys while privately suspecting they were designed by a committee of haunted printers.

## Concrete meaning

Punkia is not random mockery.

It attacks the language, rituals, and myths that make bad systems sound intelligent, fair, innovative, or inevitable.
