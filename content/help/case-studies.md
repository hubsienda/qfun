# Case Study 1: Building-material manufacturer entering a new market

## Situation

A manufacturer of a low-thickness wall insulation and restoration system wants to explore opportunities in a target country.

The product is relevant for damp walls, mould, condensation, thermal bridges, renovation, façades, and indoor comfort.

The company already has one existing regional representative in the target country but wants to understand where and how to prioritise commercial action.

## Business profile context

Sector:

Building materials, thermal insulation, wall restoration, damp and mould remediation, energy-efficiency solutions for buildings.

Products and services:

Low-thickness wall insulation and restoration system, damp-wall recovery, anti-mould and anti-condensation wall treatment, vapour-control and humidity-management solution, adhesives, skim coats, primers, finishes, and technical installation support.

Target countries:

Target Country.

Target customer types:

Building-material distributors, insulation wholesalers, renovation contractors, damp-remediation specialists, architects, energy-efficiency consultants, installers, property managers, hotel property owners, and public-building maintenance departments.

Target channels:

Regional distributors, technical representatives, installer networks, architectural specification channels, renovation contractors, trade fairs, technical training events, and public tender channels.

## Example request

Market question:

Which regions and commercial channels in the target country should be prioritised for the distribution and specification of this low-thickness wall insulation and damp/mould remediation system?

Commercial objective:

Distributor discovery.

## What QOOBIX should help identify

QOOBIX should help identify:

1. Regions where renovation, damp, hospitality maintenance, or energy-efficiency activity may create demand.
2. Distributor and installer channels worth validating.
3. Customer types that may influence adoption.
4. Competitor and substitute categories.
5. Risks that should be checked before outreach.
6. Practical next actions.

## Useful outputs

DOCX report:

Best for management review in Microsoft Word.

XLSX workbook:

Best for organising channels, regions, risks, and verification actions in Microsoft Excel.

RTF report:

Best for Google Docs.

CSV export:

Best for Google Sheets.

## How the client should use the report

The client should use the report to create a shortlist of regions, distributors, technical partners, and verification actions.

The report should not be used as a final list of confirmed partners. Every organisation, claim, competitor, and opportunity must be verified.

# Case Study 2: Competitor mapping before entering a target country

## Situation

A construction-material company wants to understand the competitor and substitute landscape in a target country before approaching distributors.

The company does not yet know whether it should position the product against insulation boards, damp-remediation products, anti-mould coatings, renovation systems, or specialist energy-efficiency solutions.

## Example request

Market question:

Which direct competitors, substitute systems, local alternatives, and incumbent solutions should be mapped before positioning this low-thickness wall insulation and damp-remediation system in the target country?

Commercial objective:

Competitor mapping.

## What QOOBIX should help identify

QOOBIX should help identify:

1. Direct competitor categories.
2. Substitute products.
3. Local incumbent solutions.
4. Buyer workarounds.
5. Positioning risks.
6. Claims that need technical validation.
7. Possible channel conflicts.
8. Verification priorities.

## How the output helps

The report can help the client avoid entering the market with a vague or weak positioning.

Instead of saying “we compete with insulation products”, the client can separate:

1. Direct technical competitors.
2. Substitute systems.
3. Lower-cost alternatives.
4. Do-nothing/status-quo behaviour.
5. Products used by contractors because they are familiar, not necessarily better.

## Recommended follow-up

After reading the report, the client should:

1. Check competitor websites.
2. Review distributor catalogues.
3. Compare technical claims.
4. Check certificates and regulations.
5. Speak with installers or technical specifiers.
6. Refine sales positioning.

A producer of a specialist food product wants to enter a new country.

The company needs to identify likely routes to market before spending money on trade fairs, agents, or direct outreach.

## Example request

Market question:

Commercial objective:

Distributor discovery.

## Business profile fields that matter

Specialist food product, including product format, target buyer, shelf-life, packaging, and price positioning.

Target countries:

The specific country to analyse.

Target customer types:

Importers, wholesalers, speciality retailers, food-service buyers, gourmet shops, regional distributors, hospitality suppliers, and online retailers.

Target channels:

Food distributors, importers, trade fairs, speciality retail, restaurant supply, gourmet e-commerce, and regional wholesalers.

Known competitors:

Existing brands, substitute products, supermarket alternatives, local producers, and imported products.

## What QOOBIX should help identify

QOOBIX should help identify:

1. Distribution channels worth validating.
2. Buyer types to prioritise.
3. Competitive alternatives.
4. Regional or segment opportunities.
5. Positioning angles.
6. Practical outreach steps.

## How the client should use the report

The client should use the output to prepare a first validation campaign.

The report should not replace direct buyer conversations, price testing, importer checks, logistics review, labelling review, or regulatory validation.

# Case Study 4: Hospitality property owner evaluating renovation demand

## Situation

A company selling renovation or building-performance solutions wants to understand whether hotels and hospitality property owners are a useful target segment.

## Example request

Market question:

Which hospitality property types, regions, and maintenance problems should be prioritised for positioning this building-renovation solution to hotel owners and property managers?

Commercial objective:

Regional opportunity assessment.

## What QOOBIX should help identify

QOOBIX should help identify:

1. Hospitality property types that may experience the relevant problem.
2. Regions where climate, building age, tourism density, or renovation activity may matter.
3. Commercial channels into the hospitality sector.
4. Influencers such as architects, maintenance companies, and energy consultants.
5. Risks around seasonality, procurement, and maintenance budgets.
6. Practical next actions.

## Useful follow-up

The client may use the report to decide:

1. Which regions to validate first.
2. Whether to approach hotel owners directly.
3. Whether to work through maintenance contractors.
4. Whether architects or consultants are more important.
5. Which proof points are needed before outreach.

# Case Study 6: Creating a 90-day action plan

A business wants to enter a market but does not know what to do first.

It does not need a long theoretical report. It needs a practical action list.

## Example request

Commercial objective:

Action-priority report.

## What QOOBIX should help identify

QOOBIX should help identify:

1. Immediate verification tasks.
2. Commercial priorities.
3. Partner discovery actions.
4. Competitor checks.
5. Positioning tests.
6. Channel validation steps.
7. Risks to resolve before spending money.

## How the client should use the report

The client should convert the report into a simple action plan with:

1. Owner.
2. Deadline.

4. Status.

6. Decision outcome.

The XLSX workbook or CSV export is especially useful for this.
