# Cookie Policy

## 3.1 About this Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how QOOBIX uses cookies and similar technologies.

## 3.2 What cookies are

Cookies are small files or pieces of information stored on a device when a website or application is used.

They can be used for essential functions, such as keeping a user signed in, securing access, or remembering session information.

## 3.3 Cookies used by QOOBIX

QOOBIX uses essential cookies required to provide secure access and maintain private client sessions.

These cookies may be used to:

1. Keep a client session active.

2. Control access to the private client area.

3. Protect against unauthorised access.

4. Support application security.

5. Remember necessary session state.

## 3.4 No marketing cookies at launch

At launch, QOOBIX does not use advertising cookies, behavioural tracking cookies, third-party marketing cookies, or profiling cookies.

If this changes in the future, this Cookie Policy will be updated and, where required, consent will be requested.

## 3.5 Essential cookies

Essential cookies are necessary for QOOBIX to function securely. Without them, the private access system, session handling, and protected result pages may not work properly.

## 3.6 Managing cookies

Users can block or delete cookies through their browser settings. However, blocking essential cookies may prevent QOOBIX from working correctly.

## 3.7 Changes to this Cookie Policy

Sienda Ltd may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The latest version will apply from the effective date shown above.
