description: Information about AI and automation in QOOBIX.
effectiveDate: 14 May 2026

## 1. Current version

This first public version of QOOBIX does not call an AI model.

The homepage Proteus encounter is authored/static.

The first version is designed to be fast, controlled, low-cost, and reliable.

## 2. Future AI use

Future versions may use AI-assisted or automated analysis where it adds real value.

Possible future uses may include interpreting user intent, summarising public business signals, identifying commercial patterns, scoring visible opportunity signals, drafting report language, preparing outreach angles, or structuring downloadable reports.

## 3. Proteus

Proteus is the intelligence and voice inside QOOBIX.

Proteus may later power proprietary scoring logic, report structures, question paths, and satirical diagnostic experiences.

In this public foundation, Proteus is represented through authored copy and static interaction.

## 4. Limits of AI

AI-generated or automated analysis can be incomplete, outdated, misleading, or incorrect.

QOOBIX does not guarantee that AI-generated or automated outputs are error-free.

## 5. User responsibility

You should review and verify outputs before using them commercially.

Automated analysis should be treated as a tool, not as a final authority.

Proteus is theatrical. Do not confuse theatrical confidence with certainty.

## 6. Cost-control doctrine

QOOBIX should not become a free open-ended AI playground.

AI should be used selectively, commercially, and with clear limits where future product paths justify it.
