## Minimal public site

This first public version of QOOBIX is designed as a minimal public website.

It does not provide user accounts, dashboards, saved reports, or stored user histories.

## Theme preference

The site may store a light or dark theme preference in your browser using localStorage.

This is used only to remember your display preference.

## External links

QOOBIX may link to Sienda Media and other Sienda properties.

External sites may have their own privacy terms and cookie practices.

## No AI processing in this version

This first version does not send user prompts to an AI model.

The Proteus encounter on the homepage is authored/static.

## Contact

For privacy matters, contact Sienda Ltd through the contact details published on sienda.co.uk.
