## 1. Operator

QOOBIX is operated by Sienda Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales under Company No. 08194971, with registered office at Third Floor, 207 Regent St, London W1B 3HH, United Kingdom.

Privacy contact only: privacy@siendaweblines.com

## 2. Privacy doctrine

QOOBIX is designed to minimise data.

This first public version does not create user accounts, dashboards, saved searches, customer lead libraries, stored report archives, or personal profiles.

## 3. What this version processes

This version may process limited technical information necessary to deliver the website, such as IP address, browser information, device information, security logs, hosting logs, and date/time of access.

It may also store your light or dark theme preference in your browser using localStorage.

## 4. Proteus encounter

The Proteus encounter on the homepage is authored and static.

This version does not send the selected answer to an AI model and does not generate a personalised report.

The answer selection is handled in the browser for the purpose of displaying the authored response.

## 5. Data we avoid

QOOBIX is not designed to collect named personal contacts, personal mobile numbers, private personal emails, LinkedIn personal profiles, or personal-contact enrichment data.

Future versions should continue to avoid unnecessary personal data collection.

## 6. Why limited data may be processed

Limited technical data may be processed to operate the site, maintain security, prevent abuse, diagnose technical problems, improve performance, respond to enquiries, and comply with legal obligations.

## 7. Legal bases

Where UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, the legal bases may include legitimate interests, legal obligation, performance of a contract where applicable, and consent where required for non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

## 8. Sienda Media and external purchases

Products connected to QOOBIX may be sold through Sienda Media, our online store.

External store, checkout, payment, tax, invoice, and transaction processes are handled outside QOOBIX and are governed by the relevant external policies.

QOOBIX does not process card payments directly.

## 9. Sharing data

We may use service providers necessary to operate the website, such as hosting, infrastructure, security, and technical service providers.

We do not sell user accounts, because QOOBIX does not create user accounts.

We do not sell stored lead libraries, because QOOBIX does not maintain stored lead libraries.

## 10. International transfers

Some service providers may process data outside the UK or European Economic Area.

Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used, such as adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, or equivalent protections.

## 11. Retention

QOOBIX is designed not to retain generated reports in this version, because this version does not generate reports.

Technical logs, security records, support communications, and accounting-related records may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

## 12. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, request portability, or complain to a supervisory authority.

To contact us about privacy matters, use: privacy@siendaweblines.com

## 13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the service.

No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 14. Children
