## 1. Permitted use

You may use QOOBIX to explore authored satirical content, read public pages, approach Da QOOBIX, follow territory paths, and discover products or future experiences connected to Sienda Media.

Future report or diagnostic features, if added, may be used only for lawful business, research, satire, learning, or commercial planning purposes.

## 2. Prohibited use

You must not use QOOBIX to request, generate, collect, sell, or misuse personal-contact databases.

You must not use QOOBIX to target private individuals, collect personal mobile numbers, private emails, named decision-makers, or social media profiles.

You must not use QOOBIX for spam, harassment, phishing, deception, impersonation, fraud, abusive outreach, unlawful discrimination, doxxing, stalking, surveillance, or reputation attacks.

You must not use QOOBIX to create misleading claims about businesses or people.

You must not overload, attack, scrape, reverse-engineer, interfere with, or attempt to bypass QOOBIX systems.

You must not resell QOOBIX outputs as guaranteed leads, exclusive databases, verified buyer intent, or certain commercial opportunity.

You must not violate Google, OpenAI, website, platform, data-source, or applicable legal rules.

## 3. Satirical content

You must not present QOOBIX satire as factual evidence about specific people or organisations unless independently verified and clearly supported.

## 4. Enforcement

We may block, refuse, suspend, or limit use of QOOBIX where we believe this policy has been violated or where a request creates unacceptable legal, technical, ethical, or commercial risk.

## 5. Future functionality

If interactive, AI-assisted, report-generation, or access-code features are added later, this policy may be expanded.
