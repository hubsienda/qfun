## Use the site lawfully

Do not use QOOBIX for unlawful, abusive, deceptive, harmful, or rights-infringing activity.

## No attacks

Do not attempt to interfere with, overload, scrape aggressively, compromise, or reverse engineer the site.

## No misuse of satire

Satirical content should not be represented as factual claims about specific people or organisations unless clearly supported elsewhere.

## Future functionality

If interactive or AI-assisted features are added later, this policy may be expanded.
